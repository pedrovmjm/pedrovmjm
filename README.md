## The three personalities behind my name

My name is long enough to look like an entire squad.  
So I accepted the truth: each part of it became a different software engineering personality.

### 🟢 Pedro

Pedro is the responsible one.

He cares about architecture, tests, observability, structured logs, security, documentation, CI/CD, versioning, governance, and everything that keeps a system alive after the first sprint.

Pedro opens pull requests calmly, writes context, thinks about edge cases, and still asks:

> “Is this scalable? Is it secure? Is it observable?”

Pedro is the side that tries to do things right from the beginning.

### 🟡 Marcelino

Marcelino believes in Go Horse.

He knows technical debt exists.  
He knows that decision will charge interest later.  
He knows the design pattern could be better.  
He knows that `TODO` may never be seen again.

But then he looks at the deadline, looks at the backlog, looks at the team and says:

> “We refactor later.”

Marcelino ships.  
Sometimes beautifully.  
Sometimes just working.  
But he ships.

He doesn't hate best practices.  
He just believes that, sometimes, the deploy also has feelings.

### 🔴 Victor

Victor is controlled chaos.

If something urgent needs to be fixed, he fixes it.  
If a weird bug needs investigation, he jumps in.  
If production starts behaving like it has developed consciousness, he faces it.

Victor is not afraid of incidents.  
Victor is afraid of meetings without a clear purpose.

He speaks little, reads too many logs, keeps Grafana open, and if something needs to go down during business hours to save the day... he is not saying he would do it, but he is also not saying he would not.

> “Production is down? I got this.”
